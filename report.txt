EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This report provides a comprehensive, end-to-end audit comparing the specifications,
departments, dashboards, workflows, vehicle categories, and business rules outlined 
in "Car_Dealership_ERP_Departments_and_Dashboards.pdf" against the current codebase.

While the core foundation, role-based access control (RBAC), multi-department desk 
routing, and major workflows (Sales, Procurement, Accounting, Imports, Recon, F&I, 
Trade-Ins, Document Vault) are implemented, several specialized modules, sub-features, 
business rule enforcement gates, and Phase 3 capabilities remain to be completed.

Below is the exhaustive catalog of items yet to be implemented, organized by 
department, system module, shared feature, and business rule.

15. CRITICAL BUSINESS RULES STRICT ENFORCEMENT (PDF Section 23)
================================================================================
[ ] Rule 1: Simultaneous Double-Selling Prevention
    - Database/UI concurrency lock that prevents a vehicle from being simultaneously 
      reserved or sold by two different sales reps.

[ ] Rule 2: Mandatory Sale Approval Gate
    - A vehicle cannot be sold or released if it has not been marked "Approved for Sale" 
      by Inspection & QA.

[ ] Rule 3: Reservation Expiration Countdown
    - Automated cancellation and return to inventory when reservations exceed policy duration.

[ ] Rule 4: Discount Authorization Hierarchy
    - Enforced ceiling where discounts exceeding authorized rep limits require manager approval.

[ ] Rule 5: Purchase Approval Thresholds
    - POs exceeding capital limits must be approved by Executive Management before issuance.

[ ] Rule 6: Trade-In Valuation & Inspection Prerequisite
    - Vehicle swap cannot be executed without attached signed appraisal and inspection sheets.

[ ] Rule 7: Payment-to-Transaction Strict Binding
    - Payments cannot exist as unallocated floating deposits without specific transaction linkage.

[ ] Rule 8: Mandatory Delivery Handover Gatekeeper
    - Vehicle status cannot transition to "Delivered" unless:
      * 100% payment is verified
      * Title transfer / CMR is issued
      * Customs SGD duty is certified
      * Customer handover checklist is signed

[ ] Rule 9: Unified Vehicle Acquisition-to-Sale Historical Ledger
    - Single consolidated audit timeline for every vehicle showing complete provenance:
      Purchase -> Shipping -> Customs -> QA Inspection -> Recon -> Listing -> Sale -> Delivery.

[ ] Rule 10: Immutable Financial Audit Trail
    - Tamper-evident logging of all GL entries, voucher approvals, and journal adjustments.

[ ] Rule 11: Strict Role Authorization (RBAC)
    - Departmental boundary enforcement (already established in core).

[ ] Rule 12: Permanent Record Deletion Restriction
    - Permanent deletion disabled for standard users; soft-delete with super-admin override.
