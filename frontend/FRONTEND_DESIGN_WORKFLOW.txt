1. EXECUTIVE PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE & SCOPE
================================================================================
The platform consists of four distinct user-facing frontend surfaces sharing a 
unified design system and backend API:

1. PUBLIC-FACING WEBSITE:
   - Brand awareness, marketing, vehicle inventory discovery, specifications, 
     company story, news, event listings, careers, and contact lead generation.

2. INTERACTIVE AUTOMOTIVE SERVICES:
   - High-engagement self-service tools: AI Car Match, Loan Calculator, Multi-Car 
     Comparison, Auto Broker Sourcing, Customs Clearance Coordination, and Sell/Swap 
     Trade-in valuation.

3. CUSTOMER SELF-SERVICE PORTAL:
   - Authenticated portal for retail/corporate clients: saved vehicles, comparison 
     sheets, active quote tracking, trade-in/swap requests, broker sourcing requests, 
     customs tracking, document uploads, and profile settings.

4. INTERNAL DEALERSHIP ERP:
   - High-density back-office administrative console for staff and management: 
     Inventory control, Sales CRM, Import & Shipping Logistics, Trade-in Valuations, 
     Financials/Accounting, Approvals, HR & Roles, and Website CMS.

2. COLOR SYSTEM & DESIGN TOKENS SPECIFICATION
================================================================================
In accordance with the latest user instructions, the entire frontend strictly 
uses the following 3 luxury colors with zero deviations:

1. #101820 (Deep Obsidian / Dark Gunmetal):
   - Role: Primary Canvas, Main Dark Background, Deep Sidebar Base, Shell Container, Card Surfaces.
   - Tailwind Class: bg-[#101820] / text-[#101820] / border-[#101820]
   - CSS Variable: var(--color-dark) / var(--background) / var(--card)

2. #F5F5F5 (Alabaster White / Off-White):
   - Role: Primary Typography, Headings, High-contrast Badges, Contrast Surfaces.
   - Tailwind Class: text-[#F5F5F5] / bg-[#F5F5F5] / border-[#F5F5F5]
   - CSS Variable: var(--color-light) / var(--foreground)

3. #C5A059 (Vegas Gold / Luxury Warm Brass):
   - Role: Primary Accent, Active Tabs, CTA Buttons, Trend Peaks, Sparklines, Glows, Borders.
   - Tailwind Class: text-[#C5A059] / bg-[#C5A059] / border-[#C5A059]
   - CSS Variable: var(--color-gold) / var(--accent) / var(--border)

* Note: #B83232 (Red) and #343A40 (Charcoal) were removed completely from the application 
per explicit user requests.

Typography Stack: Inter, Helvetica Neue, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
Border Radii: Rounded-sm (2px), Rounded-md (6px), Rounded-lg (8px), Rounded-xl (12px)
Grid System: 12-column responsive layout, 4px baseline grid

PHASE 4: INTERACTIVE AUTOMOTIVE SERVICES & CALCULATION ENGINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 4.1: AI Car Match Tool (`/services/ai-car-match`)
  - Step 1 (Preferences): Budget range, preferred category, brand affinities.
  - Step 2 (Usage & Lifestyle): Daily commute, family size, performance vs fuel 
    efficiency priority, off-road requirements.
  - Step 3 (Condition & Tech): New vs foreign-used preference, EV interest.
  - Matching Results Screen:
    * Top 3 Recommended Vehicles ranked by compatibility score (e.g. 96% Match).
    * "Why this fits you" rationale cards explaining key highlights.
    * Direct actions: "Compare", "Book Test Drive", "Speak to a Concierge".
    * Instant CRM Lead Capture submission.

Step 4.2: Loan Financing Calculator (`/services/loan-calculator`)
  - Input Controls:
    * Vehicle Price (or pre-filled from selected car)
    * Cash Down Payment / Deposit amount slider
    * Loan Term (12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72 months)
    * Interest Rate slider (%)
    * Currency toggle ($ USD / ₦ NGN)
  - Formula Engine:
    M = P * [r(1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n - 1]
  - Real-Time Output Dashboard:
    * Estimated Monthly Repayment (M)
    * Total Principal Financed (P)
    * Total Interest Payable
    * Total Loan Cost
    * Interactive Amortization Chart (Principal vs Interest breakdown over time).
  - Lead Submission: "Submit for Pre-Qualification" enquiry form capturing customer 
    details into CRM.

Step 4.3: Vehicle Comparison Tool (`/services/compare`)
  - Dynamic vehicle comparison matrix supporting 2 to 4 vehicles simultaneously.
  - Add/remove vehicle slots with search autocomplete.
  - "Highlight Differences" toggle switch.
  - Side-by-side spec rows: Price, Mileage, Engine, HP, Fuel, Transmission, Warranty, 
    Condition, Safety features.
  - Export comparison as PDF or shareable link.

Step 4.4: Auto Broker Sourcing Concierge (`/services/auto-brokers`)
  - Customer Sourcing Request Form:
    * Target vehicle specifications (Make, Model, Year range, Trim, acceptable colors).
    * Budget ceiling (including shipping & customs expectations).
    * Preferred sourcing market (USA, Europe, UAE, Japan, Local).
    * Urgency / target delivery timeframe.
  - Sourcing Status Tracker: View quotes, broker notes, and inspection reports.

Step 4.5: Customs & Logistics Specialist Portal (`/services/customs-specialists`)
  - Public informational guide on import regulations, duties, and port procedures.
  - Customs Clearance Request Form:
    * Vehicle VIN/Chassis, Bill of Lading, Purchase invoice upload.
    * Country of Origin and Shipping Port.
  - Indicative Duty & Clearance Fee Estimator.
  - Document verification submission.

Step 4.6: Sell or Swap Vehicle Trade-In Engine (`/services/sell-or-swap`)
  - Mode Switcher: "Sell My Car" vs "Swap My Car".
  - Sell Flow:
    * Step 1: Vehicle details (Year, Make, Model, VIN, Mileage, Condition).
    * Step 2: Upload vehicle exterior/interior photos and registration papers.
    * Step 3: Expected price and preferred inspection branch.
  - Swap Flow:
    * Step 1: Select desired car from current dealership inventory.
    * Step 2: Submit current trade-in car details & photos.
    * Step 3: Real-Time Swap Calculation Preview:
      Target Vehicle Price - Trade-in Estimated Value + Approved Fees = Balance Payable.
    * Step 4: Schedule inspection appointment.

6. BUSINESS CONTROLS & VALIDATION RULES MATRIX
================================================================================
- Double-Booking Prevention: Once a vehicle is marked "Reserved" or "Sold", the 
  public "Book a Test Drive" or "Buy Now" button must immediately disable.
- Reservation Expiry Timer: Active reservations must display an expiry timestamp 
  with auto-release trigger if the deposit is not verified within 48 hours.
- Margin Visibility Guard: Purchase cost, customs costs, and landed margins must 
  be strictly hidden from public views and non-finance sales roles.
- Loan Calculator Disclaimer: Mandatory warning badge: "Estimates only. Actual terms 
  subject to financing partner approval."
