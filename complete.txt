  * Rule 1 (Simultaneous Double-Selling Prevention): Real-time checkout concurrency lock warning modal 
    preventing concurrent reservation/sale of the same vehicle, displaying active agent and lock timer.
  * Rule 2 (Inspection Sign-Off Requirement): Vehicles cannot transition to "Available" showroom stock 
    without certified Intake QA diagnostic score >=90% and signed inspector certificate.
  * Rule 3 (48-Hour Vehicle Reservation Expiration): Automated 48-hour expiration timer on reservations 
    with automatic release back to active floor stock.
  * Rule 4 (Discount Approval Limits): Strict 3% sales discount ceiling; any discount >3% triggers 
    mandatory executive dual-authorization approval workflow.
  * Rule 5 (High-Value Purchase Dual Approval): Vehicle acquisitions >= $100,000 / ₦150,000,000 automatically 
    require executive management sign-off in the Procurement and Admin approval queues.
  * Rule 6 (Trade-In Valuation Prerequisite): Vehicle swap equity cannot be credited without an uploaded 
    and certified 150-point physical inspection diagnostic report.
  * Rule 7 (Transaction-to-Payment Strict Binding): Every payment receipt and bank credit is strictly 
    bound to a valid Sales Order ID or Invoice ID before general ledger posting.
  * Rule 8 (Mandatory Delivery Handover Gatekeeper): Mandatory 4-point gatekeeper modal verifying 100% financial 
    settlement, customs SGD duty clearance, signed PDI checklist, and Title transfer before gate pass issuance.
  * Rule 9 (Unified Historical Ledger / Complete Provenance): Complete 8-stage historical provenance timeline 
    from initial international acquisition PO through ocean transit, customs clearance, QA inspection, 
    reconditioning, showroom floor listing, sales agreement, and PDI delivery with cryptographic WORM hash.
  * Rule 10 (Immutable Financial Audit Trail): WORM-compliant general ledger audit trail with SHA-256 cryptographic 
    hashes, timestamp, user ID, IP address, and offsetting journal entry reversals (no destructive overwrites).
  * Rule 11 (Strict Separation of Accounting and Finance Desks): Accounting (GL, receivables, payables, tax, audit) 
    and Finance (capital allocation, treasury, credit facilities, foreign currency hedging) desks are strictly segregated 
    into separate RBAC portals, routes, and credentials.
  * Rule 12 (Permanent Record Deletion Restriction): Standard deletions perform soft-delete archiving to cold storage; 
    permanent expungement strictly gated by Super Admin dual-authorization master passphrase.
